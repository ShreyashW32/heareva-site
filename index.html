Fair questions

What does it cost?

It depends on how many calls you need and what the setup involves, so it’s worked out with you after the demo. The demo itself is free.

Can she put a keen caller straight through to me?

Not today. She saves a callback request with the time they asked for, and you ring them back.

Will she ring new leads the minute they come in?

Not on her own yet. She works from a list you give her, and only during calling hours.

Does she answer my incoming calls?

She answers the test line on this page, so you can try her. Answering your business’s own calls isn’t part of the offer yet.

Who can she ring?

People on the list you provide. It’s your list, so the rules on who your business may call still apply to you, including the Do Not Call Register.
